2. Why did I create the Czerwonak 1933–2024 project?

Czerwonak Municipality is not just a research area for me. It is the place where I live, which made the opportunity to trace the changes that have taken place here over almost a century particularly interesting to me.

My master's thesis became the starting point for creating a project that makes its results available in a more accessible and interactive form. Above all, I wanted the data and maps I had prepared not to remain solely part of an academic thesis, but to be useful to local residents and anyone interested in the history and spatial development of the municipality.

The interactive map is an extension of my research, but also an attempt to show that spatial data can be a way of telling local history.

5. Beyond the Project

The Czerwonak 1933–2024 project is one example of how I use spatial data to analyse changes taking place in the environment and landscape. My current interests, however, focus primarily on new data acquisition methods, UAV photogrammetry, remote sensing, and the reconstruction and analysis of 3D environments.

For this purpose, I combine a classical cartographic approach with modern surveying and computational techniques. The combination of different data sources — from historical maps to contemporary imagery and 3D models — is one of the directions that interests me most in geomatics.
