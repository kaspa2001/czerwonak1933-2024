How Has Czerwonak Municipality Changed?

Nine decades of land-use change can be divided into several distinctive stages.

1933

Agricultural Landscape

The beginning of the analysed period shows a municipality with a distinctly agricultural character. Arable land accounted for approximately 58% of the area of today's municipality.

Arable land 4822.65 ha
1962

The Beginning of Major Changes

Compared with 1933, the area of arable land decreased, while forest and built-up areas increased. The beginning of a long-term transformation in land-use structure becomes visible.

Forests 3099.53 ha
1965 map sheet

Industrial Development

In the following decades, industrial areas became an increasingly prominent element of the landscape. In Czerwonak, industrial facilities developed in connection with industry, agriculture and transport.

Industrial areas 101.67 ha
1992 map sheet

Accelerating Urbanisation

Between the 1965 and 1992 map sheets, the largest increase in built-up area during the entire analysed period was recorded.

Built-up areas 469.68 ha
2013

Major Recent Transformations

Between the 1992 map sheet and the 2013 study, the largest decrease in arable land and the largest increase in green areas among the analysed periods were recorded.

Arable land 2203.53 ha
2024

Suburban Municipality

The contemporary landscape of the municipality is characterised primarily by extensive forest areas, expanding built-up areas and a much smaller share of arable land than in the past.

Forests 3797.19 ha
The timeline presents the key stages of change identified based on the successive cartographic studies used in the analysis.
